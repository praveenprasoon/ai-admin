Use team deltas to find where agentic workflows help or hurt. Do not use the table as a league table: teams have different complexity, dependencies, risk, and service constraints. Investigate outliers with their engineering leads.

Sample pilot operating rule

Keep a team in the pilot when usage telemetry and human effort are measurable, the workflow preserves human approval gates, and the quality trend remains safe. If the team is blocked by environment or data access, mark the result inconclusive rather than negative.

MEASUREMENT CONTRACT

Core metric definitions

Keep the numerator, denominator, and quality criteria stable across baseline and pilot.
Throughput per FTE-week
Accepted, size-weighted units completed ÷ available engineering FTE-weeks. Use within-team story-point throughput as a secondary measure, not a cross-team currency.
Human effort per accepted unit
Total active human hours from product, analysis, engineering, QA, review, debugging, agent supervision, and rework ÷ accepted output units. Use a consistent sampling method if time tracking is incomplete.
Cycle time
Elapsed time from agreed work-start event to acceptance. Keep blocked time in the end-to-end measure; separately report waiting time and active time.
Escaped defect rate
Production defects attributable to delivered changes ÷ accepted changes × 100, using a fixed observation window and severity weighting where appropriate.
Control-adjusted change
Compare pre/post change in pilot with pre/post change in control. For rates, a useful relative estimate is (pilot post ÷ pilot pre) ÷ (control post ÷ control pre) − 1. Treat it as an estimate, not proof of causation.
Story point ruleKeep your current Jira estimates. Use each team's accepted story points per team capacity as a within-team trend, and pair it with cycle time, human effort, work mix, and quality. Never sum points across teams as if a point means the same thing everywhere.

Suggested rollout decision gates

Set thresholds with leadership before viewing results.

Ready to expand

Positive control-adjusted throughput or effort trend, repeatable across multiple teams/work types, stable or better quality, documented human approval gates, and known run cost.

Hold / investigate

More escaped defects or security issues, increased review/rework burden, results dominated by a single team, poor baseline comparability, or missing human-effort evidence.
